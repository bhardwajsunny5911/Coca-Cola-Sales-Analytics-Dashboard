# 🥤 Coca-Cola Sales Analytics Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard built to analyze Coca-Cola-inspired sales data and turn raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

The main goal of this project was not just to create charts, but to build an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that can help answer questions around sales, customers, products, delivery performance, and geographical performance.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard contains six main analytical sections:

### 1. 🔴 Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of overall business performance.

Key KPIs include:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Units Sold
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin
- Sales Growth
- Average Order Value
- On-Time Delivery %

---

### 2. 📈 Sales Analytics

Focuses on understanding sales performance and revenue trends.

Analysis includes:

- Monthly Sales Performance
- Month-over-Month Sales Growth
- Revenue vs Profit
- Sales by Product
- Sales by Channel
- Sales by Package Size
- Regional Sales Performance

---

### 3. 👥 Customer Insights

Analyzes customer-level sales performance.

Key analysis includes:

- Total Customers
- Top 10 Customers
- Customer Revenue Contribution
- Customer Sales Distribution
- Repeat Customers
- Average Sales per Customer
- Orders per Customer
- Customer Profitability

---

### 4. 🥤 Product Insights

Provides a detailed view of product performance.

Analysis includes:

- Product Sales Ranking
- Product Profitability
- Best-Selling Product
- Most Profitable Product
- Product Margin
- Sales per Unit
- Product Sales Trends
- Package Size Performance

---

### 5. 🚚 Delivery Analytics

Focuses on order fulfillment and delivery performance.

Key metrics include:

- Total Orders
- Delivered Orders
- Delayed Orders
- Cancelled Orders
- On-Time Delivery %
- Average Delivery Days
- Delivery Performance by Region
- Delivery Performance by Channel

---

### 6. 🌎 Geographical Insights

Analyzes sales performance across different regions and cities.

Analysis includes:

- Regional Sales
- Regional Profit
- Regional Customers
- Top Region
- Top City
- City Sales Ranking
- Geographical Sales Distribution
- Regional Revenue vs Profit

# 🧮 DAX & Data Modeling

The project uses DAX measures for calculating important business metrics such as:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Units
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin %
- Sales Growth %
- Average Order Value
- Customer Revenue
- Product Ranking
- Regional Performance
- On-Time Delivery %
- Month-over-Month Sales Growth

A dedicated Date Table was also created for time-based analysis.

# 🎨 Dashboard Design

I used a Coca-Cola-inspired visual theme throughout the dashboard.

### Main colors

- Coca-Cola Red: `#E50914`
- Bright Red: `#FF1A1A`
- Dark Red: `#B00000`
- Background: `#080808`
- Card Background: `#111111`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Secondary Text: `#B8B8B8`

The goal was to create a dark, clean and consistent dashboard while keeping the charts easy to read.

3. Refresh the dataset if required.
4. Use the slicers and navigation menu to explore different dashboard sections.
